Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-04

What the app accesses

Hydra GDrive requests only the Google Drive scope drive.file. With it, the app can see and manage only the files it creates itself, in a folder named "Hydra Cloud Saves" in your Drive. It cannot read any other file in your Drive. It also reads your Google account email address through the Drive API, only to show you which account is connected.

What is stored, and where

Your game save files and a small description of each save (file names, sizes, checksums, dates, the computer name) are uploaded to your own Google Drive. The sign-in token is stored encrypted on your computer. Nothing is sent to the developers of Hydra GDrive or to any server other than Google's.

Sharing

No data is collected, sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics. Google user data is used only to provide the save backup feature, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Disconnect Google Drive in the app's settings to delete the stored token, and delete the "Hydra Cloud Saves" folder from your Drive to delete the saves. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub.
