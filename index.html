Hydra GDrive

Google Drive cloud saves for Hydra Launcher

Hydra GDrive is a free, non-commercial build of the open-source Hydra Launcher (MIT license) that can back up your game save files to your own Google Drive and restore them on another computer.

It is not affiliated with Hydra Launcher or with Google.
